🐔 Email OTP Setup

Follow these steps to enable real email OTP for password reset

Quick Setup (5 minutes)

1

Create Free EmailJS Account

Go to emailjs.com and sign up for free. You get 200 emails/month free.

Open EmailJS →
2

Add Email Service

In EmailJS dashboard, go to Email Services → Add New Service. Choose Gmail, Outlook, or any email provider. Connect your email.

3

Create Email Template

Go to Email Templates → Create New Template. Use this template:

Subject: Your OTP Code - Poultry Farm Tracker

Hello,

Your One-Time Password (OTP) for password reset is:

{{otp_code}}

This OTP is valid for 5 minutes.
Do not share this code with anyone.

Regards,
Poultry Farm Tracker Team
4

Get Your Keys

Copy these 3 values from EmailJS dashboard:

  • Public Key → Account → API Keys
  • Service ID → Email Services
  • Template ID → Email Templates
5

Enter Your Keys Below

Paste your keys here and click Save. They will be stored in your browser.

Keys saved! Go to Login →

How It Works

1. User clicks "Forgot Password" and enters their registered email
2. System generates a 6-digit OTP
3. OTP is sent to their email via EmailJS
4. User enters the OTP
5. User creates a new password

Your data stays in the browser - only the OTP email is sent through EmailJS.

Important: Keep your EmailJS keys private. They are stored locally in your browser only.
